# MyMusic: Local Player Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 14, 2026**

This privacy policy explains how MyMusic: Local Player handles information in the current iPhone and iPad release. The policy is hosted in the public [MusicFree GitHub repository](https://github.com/enefry/MusicFree).

## Summary

MyMusic: Local Player is a local music player. It does not require an account, provide a streaming catalog, or send the user's imported music library to the developer.

For the current release, we do not collect, sell, or share personal information. We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services.

## Information stored on the device

The app stores information locally on the user's device so that its features work. This may include:

- Audio files selected by the user and their media metadata, such as title, artist, album, genre, duration, and artwork.
- Playlists, playlist membership, favorites, playback history, and the current playback queue.
- Playback preferences, appearance choices, language choice, and app icon choice.

This local information is used for the library, playback, playlist, and settings features. It is not collected by or sent to the developer.

## Information collected or shared

The current release does not include an account system, advertising SDK, analytics service, crash-reporting service, or tracking system. The developer does not intentionally collect or sell personal information through the app.

The app does not request contacts, location, photos, microphone access, or Apple Music access for its local-library workflow. If a future release changes these practices, this policy will be updated before that release.

## Files and user content

The user chooses which audio files to import through the system file picker or Finder file sharing. The app processes those files locally to build the library and play them. Users remain responsible for having the right to store and play the audio content they import.

## Third-party software

The app links the modified open-source [MusicFreeVLCKit](https://github.com/enefry/MusicFreeVLCKit) dynamic framework for local media processing and playback. The framework is not used by the app to collect personal information. Third-party dependency and license notices are included in the app and in the source distribution under [`ThirdPartyNotices/`](../ThirdPartyNotices/).

## Children's privacy

MyMusic: Local Player does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information in the current release, it does not create a child-specific collection profile.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released. The effective date at the top of this page will also be updated.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the public [MusicFree GitHub Issues](https://github.com/enefry/MusicFree/issues). Please do not include passwords, private keys, or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.
