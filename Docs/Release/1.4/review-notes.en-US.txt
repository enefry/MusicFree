MyMusic: Local Player is a personal audio player for iPhone and iPad. No app account or login is required for local import and playback. Users supply the audio files they have the right to use.

Version 1.4 reorganizes the native interface and settings, adds configurable song transitions and optional smart crossfades, and brings built-in audio effects and optional lyrics/music-information services together in plugin settings. It also improves translations, seeking while paused, and importing.

Local review flow:
1. In Library, import at least two audio files from Files, or transfer them with Finder file sharing.
2. Open Songs, play an imported track, and tap the mini-player to open playback controls. Test play/pause, seeking, queue, shuffle, and repeat. Pause, seek to another position, and resume.
3. Open Settings > Playback > Song transitions. Enable transitions and select crossfade; choose a duration up to 12 seconds. Smart crossfade is optional and off by default. Test with two full local tracks at normal playback speed; let playback reach the transition naturally. Smart processing may fall back to a standard transition when the audio or playback settings are unsuitable. CUE segments and network previews are not the smart-crossfade test path.
4. In Settings, open Plugins to inspect built-in effects and service controls. Equalizer and other available settings also appear in their relevant categories. Network services require the application privacy policy, the relevant service/source disclosure, and applicable plugin permissions. Local playback works without enabling online services.
5. If testing CUE support, import the CUE file together with its referenced audio file. Background the app or lock the device during local playback and test system playback controls.
6. Optional conversion is under Settings > Import and library > Audio conversion. Eligible lossless inputs can be converted to AAC-LC or ALAC. Foreground conversion can run without playback; background conversion runs only while music is playing and pauses when playback pauses, stops, or buffers.

DS Audio is an optional user-configured DSM/Audio Station source for browsing, temporary preview, and download/import. Any reviewer server and account details are provided separately in App Store Connect. Local playback does not require this service.

Only plugins included in the app are supported; there is no user interface for installing downloaded executable plugins.
